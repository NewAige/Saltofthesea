Meet Ellen Alves

200 YTT Certified

Bringing the calming energy of the ocean to the mat, Ellen provides a soulful yoga experience rooted in the Sva Ha tradition. Whether you are looking for a deep meditative flow or physical restoration, her classes are designed to reconnect you with your inner rhythm.

Ellen currently teaches at the New Bedford YMCA, guest teaches at premier South Coast studios, and offers personalized private lessons.
